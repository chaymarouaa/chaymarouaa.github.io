# Chayma Rouaa | Engineering Portfolio

Personal engineering portfolio showcasing projects, technical skills, academic background, and career objectives.

Portfolio : https://chaymarouaa.github.io/

## About

I am a Mechanical Engineering student with a strong interest in robotics, mechatronics, biomechanics, and human assistance technologies.

This portfolio brings together academic and personal engineering projects covering mechanical design, robotics, numerical modelling, simulation, programming, and prototyping.

The objective is to document both the engineering process and the technical skills developed through each project.

## Featured Projects

### Robotics and Mechatronics

- **3R Robotic Arm** : MATLAB based trajectory control of a three revolute joint robotic arm.
- **Plantigrade Robot** : Mechanical design and development of a Chebyshev linkage walking mechanism.
- **Lilium Interactive motion** : Gesture-based motion and trajectory control project.


### Numerical Modelling and Simulation

- **Granular Flow Modeling** : Discrete numerical modelling of granular flow developed in Fortran 90.
- **Crack Propagation Simulation** : Atomistic simulation of crack propagation using Python and Lennard Jones interactions.

Each project includes a concise engineering overview, technical approach, development process, results, limitations, and potential improvements.
